Our Story

Your New Coffee Corner on Myrtle Avenue

Catta is a family-run artisan café where every croissant, cake and loaf is baked right here, every single day.

Step past the green façade and wrought-iron windows into a sunlit room of brick, wood and greenery. Grab an iced latte on the way to work, share a slice of Torta della Nonna with a friend, or settle in with a book and stay awhile.
