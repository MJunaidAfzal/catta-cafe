How It Began

A Family Table on Myrtle Avenue

Catta started with a simple idea: bake everything ourselves, pour great coffee, and treat every guest like family.

Behind the green façade at 69-40 Myrtle Avenue you'll find a family that loves to feed people. Croissants are baked in-house, desserts are made by hand, and our sandwiches are built on house focaccia — every item on the menu is made here, daily.

On grand opening day the room was packed and we handed out pastry samples to anyone who walked through the door. The neighborhood has been coming back ever since.

Our Philosophy

We believe in baking it ourselves, pouring coffee with care, and making every guest feel like part of the family.
